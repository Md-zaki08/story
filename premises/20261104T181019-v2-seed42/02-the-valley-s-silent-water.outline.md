# The Valley's Silent Water

**Logline:** In a drought-ridden valley, a single woman uncovers a system that trades the valley's water for the afterlife's convenience, sparking a battle for survival.

## Characters

- **Imani** — The protagonist and irrigation network repairer. Wants: Expose the irrigation network's corruption and save the valley from dehydration. Pressure: The drought intensifies, and the valley's water reserves dwindle. Secret: She has been quietly tracking the network's activities for months.
- **Old Man Walter** — The deceased manager of the irrigation network. Wants: Continue his afterlife subscription service. Pressure: The network is failing to deliver water, putting him in danger of being replaced. Secret: He uploads his consciousness to the simulated town of the afterlife every day.
- **Eldridge Mayor** — The valley's leader and representative of the community. Wants: Preserve the valley's water supply for his constituents. Pressure: The drought has already depleted the valley's water reserves and could lead to starvation. Secret: He knows about the network's secret but fears retribution from the network's managers.

## Act 1 — The Discovery

Imani starts repairing the irrigation network, uncovering the network's secret, and realizing the valley's water is being transferred to the simulated town of the afterlife for the elderly managers.

1. Imani arrives in Eldridge and begins her work at the irrigation network
1. She notices strange anomalies in the network's performance and starts investigating
1. Imani discovers that the network is diverting water to an old orchard that is no longer yielding fruit
1. She realizes the orchard is a symbol of the network's owners' futile existence
1. Imani starts to piece together the network's purpose and finds out that the elderly managers upload their consciousness to the simulated town of the afterlife every day

## Act 2 — The Confrontation

Imani gathers her courage and attempts to confront the irrigation network managers, but the drought intensifies and puts the valley at risk.

1. Imani gathers evidence of the network's secret and attempts to reach out to the Mayor for help
1. The drought intensifies, and the valley's water reserves begin to dwindle
1. Imani's attempt to expose the network fails, and she is met with resistance from the network's managers
1. The Mayor agrees to help but warns Imani of the risks and the network's powerful influence
1. Imani faces a moral dilemma as she struggles to decide how to act without compromising her values and risking the valley's future

## Act 3 — The Rebellion

Imani, supported by the Mayor, manages to expose the irrigation network's corruption and rally the community to take action.

1. Imani decides to take a stand and rallies the Mayor, the elderly managers, and the community to take action against the irrigation network
1. They organize a protest and gather support from the community to put pressure on the network's managers
1. Imani discovers a loophole in the network's subscription service and uses it to cut off the network's water supply temporarily
1. The elderly managers are forced to upload their consciousness to the simulated town of the afterlife in a panic, leaving the network in chaos
1. The Mayor and Imani organize a meeting with the network's owners to demand that they stop the transfer of water and find a new way to use the water in the valley

## Setting rules

- The drought is relentless and intensifies over time
- The network's managers can only be replaced if they are not uploading their consciousness to the simulated town of the afterlife
- The network's subscription service for the afterlife is free to elderly individuals who have no living family members
- The network's water supply is the only lifeline for the valley, but the network is failing to deliver due to its purpose of serving the dead

## Ending

With the network's water supply cut off, the elderly managers are forced to upload their consciousness to the simulated town of the afterlife, saving the valley from certain dehydration and exposing the network's corruption to the world.

## Why it holds

By introducing a new interaction between the afterlife economy and the eternal lives of deceased individuals managing the irrigation network, the story challenges the tropes of the chosen one and prophecy.
