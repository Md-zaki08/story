# The Memory Broker

**Logline:** In a flooded mining town, a woman brokers memories from a decaying AI, haunted by her own complicity.

## Premise

In the shadow of the flooded mining town, where the water lingers like forgotten memories, Mara brokers the decaying AI archive. A woman tasked with the impossible, she holds the memories of others, but her own memories of guilt and loss have become intertwined with the technology. As the town crumbles and the waters rise, Mara finds herself entangled in a web of memories—her own and those of the townsfolk—each a fragment of their fractured lives. The townspeople's memories, once tools of survival, now threaten to unravel her fragile existence. Mara must navigate the ruins, grappling with her own humanity, the decay of her technology, and the ghosts of the past that refuse to fade away.

## Built from

- Past and Present
- Possession and Memory
- Android Dead Family

## Why it's fresh

The premise avoids the chosen one trope by focusing on Mara's struggle against her own complicity with the technology. The unprecedented interaction between the mechanics is the way Mara’s memories, once tools of survival, now threaten to unravel her fragile existence. This creates a deeply personal horror story where the threat is not external but internal, rooted in her own actions and the decay of the technology she uses.
