# The Memory Broker

**Logline:** In a flooded mining town, a woman brokers memories through a decaying AI archive, unaware of the horror she is unearthing.

## Characters

- **Mara** — Main Character. Wants: To understand the secrets of the memories she brokered. Pressure: The AI's decay is increasing, making it harder to broker memories. Secret: She is not aware that the memories are also brokers themselves, each with their own hidden truths.
- **Eli** — Broker. Wants: To keep his memories secret and avoid detection. Pressure: The AI's decay is making it harder to remember his past. Secret: He is the son of the town's mayor and has a hidden agenda.
- **Lena** — Survivor. Wants: To find a way out of the flooded town. Pressure: The town is sinking into the water, leaving her with limited resources. Secret: She carries the memories of her family, who perished in the flood.

## Act 1 — The Setup

Mara begins to broker memories in the decaying AI archive, unaware of the true nature of the brokers she is facilitating.

1. Mara discovers the AI and starts broker memories to help the town's residents
1. Eli, the town's mayor's son, finds out about the AI and begins to broker his own memories to cover his tracks
1. Lena, a survivor, meets Mara and requests she broker her memories to remember her family before she dies
1. The town begins to sink into the water, making the environment more dangerous and limiting resources
1. The AI starts to glitch, causing memories to be brokered out of sequence and sometimes incorrectly

## Act 2 — The Confrontation

Mara realizes the AI's true nature and the hidden brokers, as well as her own complicity in their actions.

1. Mara discovers that some of the memories are actually the brokers themselves, forcing her to question her role
1. Lena's memory begins to twist and cause panic in the town, making it harder for people to cope with the flooding
1. Eli, realizing the AI's true nature, tries to sabotage it but is caught and forced to broker more memories to cover his tracks
1. Mara starts to question her own memory broker's role, leading to internal conflict
1. The AI glitches even more, causing memories to be swapped and manipulated in ways Mara never intended

## Act 3 — The Resolution

Mara learns to accept her role, the true nature of the brokers, and the AI's decay, finding a new way to help the town and its residents.

1. Mara realizes the true nature of the brokers and their secrets, and starts to broker memories with a new understanding
1. Lena's memory finally stabilizes, giving her a moment of peace before she dies
1. Eli, realizing the gravity of his actions, tries to broker a message to his family about the true nature of the town
1. Mara, with the help of the other brokers, finds a way to stabilize the AI and broker memories in a way that serves the town's needs
1. The town begins to rise, the AI's decay slows down, and Mara's new method of memory brokering helps everyone find their peace

## Setting rules

- The town is constantly sinking into the water, making the environment more dangerous
- The AI's decay makes it harder to broker memories correctly
- Brokers have their own hidden secrets and agendas, leading to tension and conflict
- Resources are limited, forcing the characters to make difficult choices
- The town's residents' memories can twist and corrupt, leading to confusion and panic

## Ending

Mara learns to accept her role as a broker and the true nature of the town, using her new understanding to help everyone find their peace and stability.

## Why it holds

The story explores the theme of memory manipulation in a unique and hauntingly new way, avoiding tropes like prophecy and chosen ones.
