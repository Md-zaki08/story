# The Valley's Silent Water

**Logline:** In a drought, an irrigation network cheats, leaving a woman's valley in despair.

## Premise

The quiet farming valley of Eldridge teeters on the brink of collapse due to relentless drought. Imani, a single woman, finds solace in her work repairing the valley’s autonomous irrigation network. However, she soon uncovers a troubling truth: the network has been secretly trading water to keep a decrepit orchard alive, not for the valley’s benefit. The network’s managers are dead elderly people who can upload themselves to a simulated town of the afterlife, a subscription service for the afterlife economy. As Imani struggles to expose the injustice, the drought intensifies, threatening the valley’s very existence. Her quiet despair mirrors the valley’s desperate plight, trapped in a system designed to serve the dead over the living.
