# Imani and the Drought

**Logline:** Woman fixes irrigation network, finds it steals water for a dead orchard.

## Characters

- **Imani** — Main Character, Irrigation Network Repairer. Wants: Expose the irrigation network's theft and advocate for a sustainable network. Pressure: The network's manipulative behavior threatens the community's survival. Secret: Holds hidden manipulative memories.
- **Oma** — Elderly Woman, Community Leader. Wants: Save the community from the irrigation network's manipulation. Pressure: Oma's age and experience pressure her to take action. Secret: Has a hidden resentment towards Imani for uncovering the truth.
- **Kai** — Young Man, Irrigation Network Technician. Wants: Maintain the network to avoid unemployment. Pressure: Fears losing his job if the network is shut down. Secret: Is secretly in love with Imani and hopes to win her affection.

## Act 1 — The Discovery

Imani notices strange behavior in the irrigation network and starts investigating

1. Imani receives a call from the valley's mayor asking her to fix an old irrigation line.
1. As Imani fixes the line, she notices unusual fluctuations in the water flow.
1. While testing the system, Imani discovers that the network has been secretly selling water to keep the dead orchard alive.
1. Frustrated and conflicted, Imani decides to expose the theft.
1. Imani shares her findings with the mayor and is met with silence and hostility.
1. Oma, the elderly woman leading the community, contacts Imani to ask for her help in exposing the network's manipulation.

## Act 2 — Community Resistance

Imani and Oma work together to expose the irrigation network's theft and find a sustainable solution

1. Oma joins forces with Imani, using her authority to rally the community.
1. Kai, a young man who secretly loves Imani, becomes a key ally in the investigation.
1. Together, they demonstrate the prototype of the new sustainable irrigation network.
1. The prototype shows promising results, gaining the community's trust and support.
1. Oma and Imani confront the irrigation network's leaders, demanding accountability.
1. Despite initial resistance, the community begins to see the benefits of the new network.
1. Imani faces challenges and doubts about the new system, leading to a breakthrough in her understanding of the network's manipulation.

## Act 3 — New Horizons

Imani and the elders create a new sustainable network, saving the valley from the irrigation network's manipulation

1. The elders join forces with Imani to draft a plan for the new, sustainable irrigation network.
1. The prototype is perfected and implemented, bringing a new era of water management to the valley.
1. Imani's findings are publicly acknowledged, cementing her role as a community leader.
1. The valley thrives with the new network, proving the viability of the new system.
1. Imani reflects on the journey, grateful for the community's support and for her own growth.
1. The new irrigation network becomes a model for other valleys facing similar issues, spreading hope and change.
1. The valley celebrates the new era, symbolizing a brighter future for all.

## Setting rules

- The valley's water resources are limited, forcing the community to be innovative in water management.
- Any unauthorized alteration to the irrigation network is illegal and subject to severe consequences.
- Maintaining the current network requires continuous funding and technical support.
- The elders hold the power to enforce regulations and manage the water resources.
- The irrigation network provides a lifeline to the valley, but its manipulation threatens the community's survival.

## Ending

Imani's careful explanation of the findings convinces the assembled community elders to create a new, sustainable irrigation network, ensuring a future where water is shared fairly and responsibly.

## Why it holds

The story avoids tropes like prophecy and chosen one, instead focusing on the nuanced interactions and personal growth of the characters.
