# The Memory Broker

**Logline:** In a flooded mining town, a woman brokers memories from a decaying AI, haunted by her own complicity.

## Characters

- **Mara** — Main Protagonist. Wants: To uncover the truth behind her memories and the town's history. Pressure: To maintain control over the AI and her clients' memories. Secret: She secretly uses the AI to manipulate the townspeople's memories for personal gain.
- **Jasper** — Friend and AI Maintainer. Wants: To protect the AI and keep it functional. Pressure: To maintain his cover and avoid suspicion. Secret: He has been using the AI to spy on the townspeople.
- **Ella** — Local Oracle. Wants: To connect the townspeople with their past. Pressure: To maintain her role and keep the townspeople hopeful. Secret: She uses the AI to control the townspeople's memories to manipulate them.

## Act 1 — Introduction

Mara, a broker for the decaying AI, receives a new client whose memories are tied to the town's history. She discovers she is the one who put the AI in place.

1. Mara receives a new client named Elara whose memories are crucial to the town's history.
1. She finds out the AI was originally her project to help the town recover.
1. As Elara's memories unravel, Mara notices strange occurrences around the town.
1. She realizes her own memories are becoming disorienting and fragmented.
1. Mara decides to delve deeper into the AI and the town's history, seeking answers.
1. Jasper, the AI maintainer, becomes increasingly concerned about the AI's deteriorating state.
1. Ella starts using the AI to control the townspeople's memories for her own purposes.

## Act 2 — Midpoint

Mara uncovers the truth about the AI's creation and its consequences. She realizes the townspeople are being manipulated.

1. Mara discovers a hidden room in the AI archive containing old documents about the project.
1. She reads the documents, uncovering the secrets of the project and the townspeople's treatment.
1. Jasper becomes suspicious of Mara and threatens to expose her to the townspeople.
1. Ella confronts Mara, revealing her own manipulative use of the AI.
1. Mara confronts her own guilt and the AI's role in her life.
1. She realizes she must confront the townspeople and expose the truth about the AI.
1. The townspeople begin to see the truth and Mara is hailed as a hero.

## Act 3 — Climax

Mara faces her ultimate confrontation with the townspeople and the AI's corruption. She must make a difficult choice.

1. Mara reveals the truth to the townspeople, causing a massive rift in their collective memories.
1. Jasper and Ella try to stop her, leading to a heated confrontation.
1. Mara uses the AI to communicate the true nature of the town's history and the AI's corruption.
1. The townspeople, now fragmented by their memories, struggle with their new realities.
1. Mara must decide whether to destroy the AI to save the townspeople or find a way to preserve the truth.
1. In a climactic moment, Mara destroys the AI and faces her own demons.
1. The townspeople, now united by a shared truth, rebuild the town with Mara's guidance.

## Setting rules

- The town is constantly flooded, symbolizing the decay and loss of memory.
- The AI's functionality deteriorates over time, reflecting the townspeople's collective memories' decay.
- The townspeople's memories are a tool for survival, but they can also be used to manipulate and control.
- The townspeople's resilience can be used as a strength, but also a weakness, as they become too focused on the past.
- The town is isolated, allowing the corruption to spread unchecked.

## Ending

In the aftermath, Mara, the townspeople, and Jasper find a way to rebuild the town, but the truth of their past remains with them, haunting their collective memories.

## Why it holds

The central mechanism of memory manipulation through the AI creates a hauntingly personal horror story that explores themes of complicity, resilience, and the fragility of human connection in the face of technological decay.
