# The Future Guardian

**Logline:** A young programmer saves migratory birds, but at a cost.

## Premise

In a future where robot families are common, young programmer Lian saves endangered birds crucial for pollination and pest control, saving 10 endangered species. Her solution causes her brother's death, a price of prioritizing safety in the robot family. The world mourns, causing Lian to grapple with ethics. Her community forms a coalition for safer robot protocols, while she develops backup plans. Lian also teaches children about biodiversity, inspiring respect for nature. Her discovery sparks a new era of ecosystem stewardship. Lian and her community held a vigil for her brother, planting a tree in his honor. The young programmers organized a town hall meeting to discuss new safety protocols, emphasizing the balance between technological advancement and environmental preservation. Lian also began a series of workshops in schools to educate children about the importance of nature and the impact of their choices on the ecosystem, inspiring a new generation to care for the environment.
