Nier: Automata is a 2017 action role-playing game developed by PlatinumGames and published by Square Enix. It
is the sequel to Nier (2010). Nier: Automata was originally released for the PlayStation 4 and Windows via
Steam. It was ported to Xbox One in June 2018, and Nintendo Switch in October 2022. Nier: Automata is set
during a proxy war between alien-created Machines and human-crafted androids, focusing on the actions of
combat android 2B, scanner android 9S, and rogue prototype A2. The story requires multiple playthroughs, with
each playthrough revealing new story elements. The gameplay combines role-playing elements with action-based
hack-and-slash combat. It also includes gameplay from other video game genres, with elements varying from
shoot 'em up to text adventure. Production began in 2014, with series creator Yoko Taro, producer Yosuke
Saito, and lead composer Keiichi Okabe reprising their roles in Nier. Akihiko Yoshida, known for his work in
the Final Fantasy series, led character design. The goal was to make a sequel that would be faithful to Nier
while improving the combat system. Because the project was new to PlatinumGames, its staff faced multiple
challenges when developing the gameplay and open-world environment. The story, written by Yoko, explores
themes of finding value in life and the reasons people kill. Automata was localized by 8-4, translators of
Nier. The game was formerly unveiled at E3 2015. Nier Automata received widespread acclaim from critics who
praised the game's story, themes, gameplay, music, visuals, and characters, with criticism directed at its
presentation for some technical problems. The PC release drew a mixed response due to technical problems that
were not addressed until 2021. The Nintendo Switch release was praised as one of the best third-party games on
the system, although some reviewers criticized its downgraded graphics and frame-rate drops. Since release,
Nier: Automata has been widely considered to be one of the greatest video games of all time, and its
soundtrack one of the greatest ever produced. In addition, the game was a commercial success, selling over 10
million copies worldwide as of February 2026. Automata's strong sales and reception were credited for saving
its developer PlatinumGames, with the company prior to the game's release facing a severe financial crisis
after several years of disappointing game releases. Nier Automata would receive stage plays and novels
expanding its narrative, an anime adaptation, and both downloadable content (DLC) and crossovers with other
games. Nier: Automata is an action role-playing game in which players explore an open world. In addition to
standard navigation on foot, the game features scenarios with flying mech battles, as well as a special item
that can summon wild animals to ride. Non-playable characters (NPCs) found throughout the world offer quest
objectives, and shops in hub locations sell various items, including consumables that recover health. Combat
uses a real-time action-based hack and slash system, using both light and heavy attacks and combining them
into different combination attacks. The player can also evade incoming attacks, gaining invulnerability or
counterattack bonuses based on timing. The player is assisted by a flying "Pod" robot which launches
