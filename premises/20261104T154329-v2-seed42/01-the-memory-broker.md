# The Memory Broker

**Logline:** In a flooded mining town, a woman brokers memories through a decaying AI archive, unaware of the horror she is unearthing.

## Premise

In the decaying ruins of a flooded mining town, the only light comes from a flickering screen where memories are brokered through a decaying AI archive. Mara, a woman who deals with the dark side of human memory, learns that the people she is archiving are also brokers, each with their own secrets. As the town sinks into darkness, Mara finds herself not only brokered by the AI but also by the memories it uncovers, leading her to question the very nature of humanity and the boundaries of technology.

## Built from

- Memory Replay
- Intimacy by Proxy

## Why it's fresh

In this story, Mara is not a chosen one but a broker of memories, forcing a fresh perspective on the trope of a chosen one. Additionally, the unique interaction between the decaying AI and the human brokers of memories creates a hauntingly new scenario within the horror genre.

**Judge:** coherence 7.0/10, freshness 7.0/10, viability 6.0/10. The engine is clear and coherent, and the story is fresh with the combination of Mara, the flooded mining town, and the decaying AI archive.
