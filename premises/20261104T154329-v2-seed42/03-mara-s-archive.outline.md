# Mara's Archive: A Woman's Descent

**Logline:** In a flooded mining town, a woman brokers memories through a dying AI archive, battling a malevolent presence and rising floodwaters.

## Characters

- **Mara** — Main Protagonist. Wants: Reclaim her identity and control the memories in the AI archive.. Pressure: The malevolent presence in the AI archive threatens to consume her.. Secret: She is the only one who knows how to turn off the AI archive's power, but it means losing her connection to others..
- **Ethan** — Brokeman. Wants: Preserve his memories and prevent his identity from being lost.. Pressure: Mara's desperation to control the archive is overwhelming.. Secret: He is not who he seems; he's using his memories to control the situation..
- **Rachel** — The Other Brokeman. Wants: Find a way to save the town from the flood.. Pressure: Mara's memory manipulation is threatening the town's cohesion.. Secret: She knows the real cause of the flood and is using it to her advantage..

## Act 1 — The First Encounter

Mara is called in to broker memories for a mysterious stranger. The first memories resurface, and Mara realizes her own memories are at risk.

1. Ethan arrives at Mara's home, bringing his memories to broker.
1. Mara begins to broker Ethan's memories, feeling a strange connection.
1. The first memory surfaces, showing a distant past event.
1. Mara realizes her own memories are fading as she focuses on Ethan's.
1. Rachel arrives, hinting that her own memories might be connected to the flood.
1. Mara discovers a hidden room in her home, housing the dying AI archive.
1. Mara confronts Ethan about his memories, suspecting him of being more than a broker.

## Act 2 — The Rising Tension

As memories continue to surface, Mara's struggle with the AI archive intensifies. The malevolent presence grows stronger.

1. Mara starts to feel like her own memories are being manipulated by the AI.
1. Ethan's memories start to reveal disturbing truths about the town's history.
1. Rachel's memories hint at a connection to the town's flooding.
1. Mara discovers a hidden chamber within the AI archive, filled with ancient, corrupted memories.
1. Ethan's memories reveal a prophecy of a flood, leading Mara to believe he's part of it.
1. The floodwaters start to rise, threatening to engulf the town and the AI archive.
1. Mara tries to save the town but the AI archive's power is her only hope against the malevolent presence.

## Act 3 — The Final Stand

Mara must confront her own fears and the malevolent presence to save herself and the town. The truth about the flood and the AI archive is revealed.

1. Mara discovers the hidden room in her home, filled with ancient, corrupted memories.
1. She confronts Rachel and learns her memories are connected to the town's flooding.
1. Mara realizes the truth about Ethan and realizes he is a pawn in the malevolent presence's scheme.
1. Mara must decide whether to use the AI archive's power to save herself or to destroy it and save the town.
1. The malevolent presence is defeated, but at the cost of the AI archive's power.
1. Mara finds a way to reconnect with others through a new means, preserving her own identity and memory.
1. The floodwaters recede, and the town is saved, but at the cost of the AI archive's remnants.

## Setting rules

- The town is flooded, making the landscape a constant reminder of the disaster.
- The AI archive's power is finite and can only be accessed by someone who understands its purpose.
- The memories in the archive can be manipulated, but this manipulation comes at a cost.
- The malevolent presence in the archive cannot be completely defeated without understanding its true nature.
- The town's flooding is not a coincidence, and the AI archive holds the key to unlocking the truth.

## Ending

Mara learns to connect with others through new means, preserving her own identity and memory without the need for the dying AI archive.

## Why it holds

The story explores themes of memory manipulation, resilience, and the dangers of technology, set in a unique and immersive flooded mining town setting.
