# Mara's Archive

**Logline:** In a flooded mining town, a woman brokers memories to save her own identity.

## Premise

In a decaying mining town drowned by floods, Mara brokers memories through a dying AI archive. Her latest broker is a stranger who copied his memories into her home, so two mourners share a dead person’s archive. As memories from the past resurface, Mara struggles to reclaim her own identity from the malevolent presence in the decaying archive. The floodwaters rise, threatening to engulf everything, including Mara’s desperate quest to piece together her fragmented life.

## Why it's fresh

This premise avoids the tropes of a chosen one and prophecy, presenting a unique interaction between the mechanics of sharing memories and the concept of a dying AI archive. Mara’s desperate quest to reclaim her identity creates a fresh narrative, as she grapples with the malevolent presence in the decayed archive and the encroaching floodwaters.
